# Illustrative Sample: General Prior-Authorization Documentation Guidelines

> ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE TEXT FOR AN EDUCATIONAL PROTOTYPE. This is not a real
> payer policy, not medical guidance, and must not be used for coverage decisions.

## Required request fields

Each request should identify the member, the requesting provider, the requested
procedure and its laterality, and the primary diagnosis.

## Clinical notes

Clinical notes should be recent, signed, and specific to the condition for
which the service is requested. Notes should state findings explicitly rather
than by reference to outside records.

## Missing or inconsistent information

When information is missing or appears inconsistent, the reviewer should
contact the provider for clarification. Absence of documentation is not
evidence that a treatment did not occur.
