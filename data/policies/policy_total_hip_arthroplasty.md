# Illustrative Sample Policy: Total Hip Arthroplasty (THA)

Applies to procedure codes: 27130

> ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE TEXT FOR AN EDUCATIONAL PROTOTYPE. This is not a real
> payer policy, not medical guidance, and must not be used for coverage decisions.

## Scope

This sample policy describes example documentation that a reviewer may look for
when a prior-authorization request is submitted for total hip arthroplasty
(hip replacement surgery).

## Criterion 1: Diagnosis

The clinical documentation should state the diagnosis supporting the hip
replacement request, such as osteoarthritis of the hip, avascular necrosis
(osteonecrosis) of the femoral head, or inflammatory arthritis. The diagnosis
on the request should match the diagnosis documented in the clinical note.

## Criterion 2: Imaging findings

The documentation should include imaging of the affected hip, such as an AP
pelvis radiograph or hip MRI, describing the findings. Example findings include
joint space loss, subchondral sclerosis, osteophytes, or femoral head collapse.

## Criterion 3: Conservative treatment

The documentation should describe nonsurgical management of hip symptoms and
the response to it. Examples include physical therapy, NSAIDs or other
analgesics, weight loss, or use of an assistive device such as a cane, with a
statement that symptoms persisted despite treatment.

## Criterion 4: Persistent symptoms and clinical rationale

The documentation should describe persistent hip or groin pain, stiffness, or
reduced mobility, their duration, and the resulting functional limitation, and
explain the clinical rationale for hip replacement.
