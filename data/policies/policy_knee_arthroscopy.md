# Illustrative Sample Policy: Knee Arthroscopy

Applies to procedure codes: 29880, 29881

> ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE TEXT FOR AN EDUCATIONAL PROTOTYPE. This is not a real
> payer policy, not medical guidance, and must not be used for coverage decisions.

## Scope

This sample policy covers arthroscopic knee surgery, such as partial
meniscectomy or meniscal repair. It does not apply to knee replacement.

## Indications

Documentation should describe a mechanical problem such as a meniscal tear with
locking or catching, confirmed by MRI of the knee. Arthroscopy is generally not
described as a treatment for osteoarthritis alone in this sample text.

## Conservative care before arthroscopy

The note should describe a trial of physical therapy, activity modification,
or anti-inflammatory medication for knee symptoms before arthroscopy, unless
the knee is mechanically locked.

## Imaging

An MRI of the knee describing the meniscal or ligament injury should be
included. Plain radiographs may be included to assess for arthritis.
