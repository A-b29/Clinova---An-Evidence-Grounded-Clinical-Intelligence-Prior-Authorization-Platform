# Illustrative Sample Policy: Lumbar Spine MRI

Applies to procedure codes: 72148

> ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE TEXT FOR AN EDUCATIONAL PROTOTYPE. This is not a real
> payer policy, not medical guidance, and must not be used for coverage decisions.

## Scope

This sample policy covers advanced imaging (MRI) of the lumbar spine for low
back pain.

## Clinical indications

Documentation should describe low back pain with radiculopathy, neurological
deficit, or red-flag symptoms such as suspected infection, malignancy, or
cauda equina syndrome.

## Conservative care

For uncomplicated low back pain, the note should describe at least six weeks of
conservative care such as physical therapy, NSAIDs, or home exercise before
advanced imaging is requested.

## Prior imaging

Plain radiographs of the lumbar spine, if performed, should be summarized.
